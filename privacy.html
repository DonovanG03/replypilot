ReplyPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. Overview

ReplyPilot is a personal Android AI assistant designed to maintain conversational context and user-controlled memories.

2. Information Processed

ReplyPilot may process user-provided messages, conversation context, saved memories, and information selected for import or backup.

3. Local Storage

ReplyPilot is designed to store its primary memory data locally on the user's device.

4. Google Drive Backups

ReplyPilot uses the Google Drive API to back up and restore memory data.

The application requests the drive.file permission, which provides access to files created by or explicitly opened with ReplyPilot.

Automatic backups are designed to be retained for seven days and then deleted by the application. This retention period applies to ReplyPilot-managed backups, not unrelated files in Google Drive.

5. AI Processing

ReplyPilot uses the OpenAI API to provide AI-powered functionality. Relevant user messages and conversation context may be transmitted to OpenAI for processing.

Future versions may offer Google Gemini as an additional AI provider. Any such integration will be disclosed when enabled.

6. Data Sharing

ReplyPilot is designed to transmit data to Google Drive for backup and restoration and to configured AI providers for requested AI processing. These services handle information under their respective terms and privacy policies.

7. User Control

Users can revoke Google Drive authorization through Google Account settings. Users can also manage ReplyPilot backup files through Google Drive.

8. Security

ReplyPilot uses Google authorization to request access to permitted Drive files. Its data handling and security controls may change as development continues.

9. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated as ReplyPilot adds features or changes its data handling. The latest version will be published here.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support email provided on the Google authorization screen.
